ADMISSION PROCEDURE
----------------------------------------------------------
Source: https://www.yaraschool.net/admission/admission-tc/
Admission is for Indian nationals, subject to seats. New-year admission is normally processed in the previous academic year's last quarter; exceptional admission requires school approval.
Application form: SAR 50 including VAT, Nursery to Grade XI; available at the office or via website download with payment on submission.
Entrance test: fresh UKG-and-up admissions; non-refundable SAR 100 including VAT.
Fresh-admission cut-offs published: June 30 for IX/XI; September 30 for lower grades. Confirm the applicable session.
Grade I admission without a TC may use a competent-authority birth certificate.
The page's general text allows direct X/XII admission subject to CBSE requirements, while its XI tab says no direct XII admission. These conflict: refer Class XII eligibility to Admissions, not an automated yes/no answer.
The nursery/LKG/UKG age table mixes a 2025 label with a 2026 reference date; do not calculate eligibility from that inconsistent table.
